About Me

I'm a student level designer focused on creating gameplay spaces built around exploration, combat, and player progression. I'm particularly interested in how level structure, alternate routes, shortcuts, and resource placement can shape player choices and experiences.

My recent work includes several Half-Life 2 level design projects created with Hammer Editor, exploring ideas such as nonlinear routes, shortcut-based progression, combat encounters, and Metroidvania-style exploration. My design process includes diagrams, sketches, blockouts, documentation, and in-engine iteration.

I also have experience with Unreal Engine and Unity, where I have prototyped gameplay systems and player interactions. Alongside my projects, I write level design and gameplay breakdowns to study how existing games guide players, structure spaces, and create different gameplay experiences.

I'm currently an undergraduate student in Computation Arts at Concordia University.
