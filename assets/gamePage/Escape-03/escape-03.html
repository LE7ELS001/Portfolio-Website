Overview

This is a Half-Life mod project centered around creating a Metroidvania-style level, where players progressively acquire new weapons and abilities that allow them to access previously unreachable areas and rooms. The level uses weapon acquisition and ability progression to encourage players to continue exploring and progressing through the level, while resource placement encourages players to revisit previously explored areas. Throughout the level design process, I used methods such as diagrams, sketches, mapping tools, and documentation to plan and develop the level.

How to Play

This level is provided as a .bsp file.

Place the file into:
Steam\steamapps\common\Half-Life 2\hl2\maps

Then launch Half-Life 2 and type:
map escape_03
in the console.
