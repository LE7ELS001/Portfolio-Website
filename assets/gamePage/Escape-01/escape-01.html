Overview

This is a Half-Life 2 mod project centered around creating a level with multiple routes to achieve the objective, giving players different ways to approach the level and providing varied experiences through exploration. The design aims to encourage replayability by allowing players to discover and experience different routes. Throughout the development process, I primarily used shooting and physics-based mechanics to create combat and puzzle-solving experiences. I also used methods such as sketches and documentation to plan and develop the level.

How to Play

This level is provided as a .bsp file.

Place the file into:
Steam\steamapps\common\Half-Life 2\hl2\maps

Then launch Half-Life 2 and type:
map escape_01
in the console.
